I am pleased to announce that I have joined [42dot](https://42dot.ai) in Silicon Valley as a Distinguished Scientist.

In this new role, I will contribute my experience, expertise, and dedication to advancing connectivity for the next generation of Physical AI platforms — from intelligent machines and vehicles to the cloud infrastructure that supports them.

Dr Minwoo Park, who recently joined 42dot as CEO, is assembling an exceptionally talented team with an ambitious vision for the future of software-defined mobility and Physical AI. I am honoured to be part of it and excited about what we will build together.
